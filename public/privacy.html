DevTrack Privacy Policy

DevTrack is a project and task tracking system used for managing projects, tasks, development team assignments, and project documents.

Google Drive Access

DevTrack uses Google Drive access to create project folders and upload, organize, and manage project-related files.

Data Usage

Information accessed through Google Drive is used only to provide the file management features of DevTrack and is not sold to third parties.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the DevTrack administrator.
